# Meeting Room Booker

A team member picks a date, sees open rooms, and reserves a 30-minute slot. Cancelling their booking puts the slot back on the schedule.

**First release.** Find an available room, book it, and cancel your own booking. Calendar sync can wait.

**The rule that matters.** When two people ask for the same room and slot, only one booking succeeds. The other person sees the conflict and can choose a different slot.

This is a fictional viewer demo with selected blueprint files. Session and review entries illustrate what recorded evidence looks like. Both build milestones are pending.
