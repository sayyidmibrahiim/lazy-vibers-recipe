## Must fix

- [major] RV-L01-f3dc2e5c · L01 · L01.1 · open · `.lvr/docs/sections/S2-kondisi.md#id:D-011`: Fails if notary/PPAT and insurance estimates have no default value or source: D-011 adds "notary/insurance" to upfront cash, but no figure, range or cite is recorded anywhere. If the app starts them at Rp0, the F-02 drop rule lets through houses whose real upfront cash is higher than the cash available. Fix (decision): Decide: (a) research sourced default estimates (notary/PPAT fee, life and fire insurance) and record them with cites; or (b) ship no default: until the owner enters them, upfront cash shows "belum lengkap" and no house is marked as passing on cash.
- [major] RV-L02-47261332 · L02 · L02.2 · open · `.lvr/docs/sections/S4-features.md#id:F-01`: Fails when the CSV is saved with Indonesian settings: Excel and LibreOffice set to Indonesian save CSV with ";" as the separator, and prices are often written "Rp1.500.000.000" or "1,5 M". F-01 lists the columns but not the separator, encoding or number format, so a builder may read one column per row or parse 1.500.000.000 as 1,5. Fix (mechanical): Add one line to F-01: accept "," or ";" (auto-detected), UTF-8 with or without BOM, and prices as plain digits, "Rp1.500.000.000" or "1,5 M"; anything else is a rejected row with its reason.
- [major] RV-L02-b541707a · L02 · L02.4 · open · `.lvr/docs/sections/S4-features.md#id:F-02`: Fails when the owner's age limits the tenor: an expert's first question is "how old will you be when the loan ends?". BCA allows at most 25 years and the borrower may be at most 65 (employee) or 66 (self-employed) at loan end. The plan uses a fixed 20-year tenor with no age rule, so for a 50-year-old owner the real tenor is 15 years and the real installment about 20% higher than shown; a house passes that the bank will refuse. Fix (decision): Decide: (a) add "usia saat ini" (stored locally) and a per-bank "usia maksimal saat lunas" (default 65), and cap the tenor at that age; or (b) keep tenor manual and show a warning line next to the tenor field about the age-at-loan-end rule.
- [major] RV-L10-31d97b4b · L10 · L10.1 · open · `.lvr/docs/sections/S5-research.md#id:D-050`: Fails when the builder starts: the plan says to use the distro Python 3.14.4 and Django 6.1.3 but gives no install line and no virtual environment. Under PEP 668 (Final) a distro-managed Python refuses a global "pip install django" (externally-managed-environment), so the start command fails on day one. Fix (mechanical): Add the install line to S5 and the handover: python3 -m venv .venv; .venv/bin/pip install "Django==6.1.3"; start with .venv/bin/python manage.py runserver 127.0.0.1:8000. Fallback when PyPI is unreachable: a saved wheel in the project folder.
- [minor] RV-L01-be07777c · L01 · L01.5 · open · `.lvr/docs/sections/S2-kondisi.md#prices-quoted`: Fails when the loan is above Rp500 juta: the plan lists only the Rp500.000 administration fee "loans up to Rp500 juta"; the BCA page today also lists 0,10% of the loan above Rp500 juta up to Rp3 miliar and Rp3.000.000 above. A Rp1,2 miliar loan (typical in South Jakarta) pays Rp1.200.000, not Rp500.000, so upfront cash is understated. Fix (research): Add the two higher BCA administration tiers (0,10% of loan above Rp500 juta to Rp3 miliar; Rp3.000.000 above Rp3 miliar) to Prices quoted, U-05 and the F-03 default figures, with today's cite.
- [minor] RV-L01-c4a7d48b · L01 · L01.5 · open · `.lvr/docs/research/domain-brief.md#id:U-01`: Fails if the LTV end date is wrong: the primary page cited for U-01 (PADG 30/2025) says the relief runs "sampai dengan tanggal 31 Desember 2026", while the plan says 31 Dec 2027 (old value 31 Dec 2027, value on the primary page today 31 Dec 2026). The 2027 date rests only on a news report; the newer regulation was not read. Fix (research): Find and read the newer BI regulation (PADG 2026) that extends the relief; cite it as the primary source in U-01, R-9 and D-010, or change the date to 31 Dec 2026.
- [minor] RV-L04-e7e37a89 · L04 · L04.2 · open · `.lvr/review/runs/run-<N>/scope-proposal.md#id:F-01`: Fails if a feature turns out not to help: the proposal names each feature's purpose but no kill criterion, so nothing says what result would drop F-03 or F-05 after the first month. Fix (decision): Add one kill line per feature in the S7 record, e.g. F-05: "dropped if no restore is needed in 6 months"; F-03: "dropped if the owner never edits a default".
- [minor] RV-L15-2e67bd28 · L15 · L15.1 · open · `.lvr/docs/sections/S1-context.md#id:D-004`: Fails if success cannot be measured: D-004 says "faster filtering" without a baseline time or a target, and gives no way to check "no unaffordable house" after use.
