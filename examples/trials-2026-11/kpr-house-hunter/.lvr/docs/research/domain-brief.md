# Domain Brief

- Idea (EN): A personal, local-only tool that filters a listings file exported from Indonesian property sites into a ranked shortlist that fits the owner's KPR (home mortgage) limits.
- Market: Indonesia (S0.q4)
- Built: 2026-11-26 · Research log: RQ-002…RQ-012
- Status: complete

## Common failure modes and pitfalls

| ID | Finding (EN) | Why it matters | Source | Section | Became |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U-08 | Low KPR rates are fixed only for the first 1–5 years; after that BCA charges its floating rate, currently 11% and reviewed every 6 months, while its regular 1-year fixed rate is 7,00%. | An affordability check at the promo rate can pass houses whose installment jumps sharply after the fixed period. | [cite: price · https://www.bca.co.id/id/individu/produk/pinjaman/kpr/kpr-first · n.d. · acc 2026-11-26 · primary · live] | S4 | S4.q2, D-035 |
| U-09 | Legal status traps: SHGB titles expire and must be renewed while SHM does not; an AJB alone is a sale record, not a title; unbuilt (inden) houses rely on a PPJB, and buyers are told to check the developer on SiReng, the land status and when the SHM will be issued. | A cheap listing can be cheap because of its title or build status; a shortlist that ranks on price alone pushes the riskiest houses to the top. | [cite: general · https://www.detik.com/properti/tips-dan-panduan/d-8238944/mau-beli-rumah-yang-belum-dibangun-cek-8-hal-ini-dulu-biar-nggak-ketipu · pub 2025-12-02 · acc 2026-11-26] | S4 | D-033 |
| U-10 | Banks check every applicant's credit history in OJK's SLIK; since mid-2026 OJK no longer shows debts under Rp1 juta, but larger paylater or online-loan arrears still appear and can block a KPR. | Affordability on paper means nothing if the credit check fails; the owner's own credit record is a precondition the tool cannot see. | [cite: regulation · https://www.fortuneidn.com/finance/slik-dilonggarkan-nasabah-nunggak-di-bawah-rp1-juta-masih-bisa-ambil-kpr-subsidi-00-ccw2k-15tp04 · pub 2026-04-14 · acc 2026-11-26 · amend-check 2026-11-26] [cite: regulation · https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/menakar-dampak-pelonggaran-aturan-slik-ojk-demi-dorong-kpr-subsidi?page=all · pub 2026-04-14 · acc 2026-11-26 · amend-check 2026-11-26] | S2 | D-013 |
