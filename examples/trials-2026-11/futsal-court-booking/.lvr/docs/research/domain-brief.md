# Domain Brief

- Idea (EN): A web app where players see open futsal court slots and reserve one; reservations live in Supabase; when two players book the same court, date and time at once, exactly one wins and the other sees "slot taken".
- Market: Indonesia (S0.q4)
- Built: 2026-11-26 · Research log: RQ-002…RQ-006
- Status: complete

## Common failure modes and pitfalls

| ID | Finding (EN) | Why it matters | Source | Section | Became |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U-08 | PostgreSQL (the database inside Supabase) runs at Read Committed by default, where each query sees only data committed before it began. Its docs warn that an insert can still hit a unique violation even after checking that the key is absent. Unique and exclusion constraints are the database rules that refuse a second conflicting row. | "Check if free, then insert" lets two players book the same slot at once; the one-winner rule must live in the database, and the loser's "slot taken" message must come from that refusal. | [cite: general · https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html · n.d. · acc 2026-11-26 · primary · live] [cite: general · https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-constraints.html · n.d. · acc 2026-11-26 · primary · live] | S4 | S4.q1, D-027 (one winner by database refusal) |
| U-09 | A 2022 field study of a futsal venue found frequent booking cancellations led the owner to take a down payment (panjar), and renters objected when it was forfeited because the owner never stated the terms or a maximum amount. | Unstated deposit and cancellation rules cause disputes; the app must show them before the player books. | [cite: general · https://e-journal.stishid.ac.id/index.php/ats-tsarwah/article/download/180/143/656 · pub 2022-08 · acc 2026-11-26 · live] | S4 | D-030 (rules shown before booking) |
| U-10 | Venues on AYO, an Indonesian sports-booking platform that says it partners with over 2,300 venues, publish strict rules such as "All bookings are final. No refunds or reschedule", and AYO's App Store reviews complain about downtime, slow loading at peak time and a convenience fee. | Players already know the platform alternative and its pain points; the owner's own site competes on fewer fees and reliability. | [cite: market · https://ayo.co.id/v/the-field · n.d. · acc 2026-11-26 · primary · live] [cite: market · https://apps.apple.com/id/app/ayo/id1539581877 · n.d. · acc 2026-11-26 · primary · live] | S1 | S1.q2 |
