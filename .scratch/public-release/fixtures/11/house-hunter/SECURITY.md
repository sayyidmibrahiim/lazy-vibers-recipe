# Access and privacy

- Grant the owner's account access to the local database and export folder. Keep income, debt, deposit limits and precise location local. A network lookup can send a broad office area and a public listing area under the workflow's allowed fields.
- Keep a named backup and test a restore on a copy. Let the owner delete their profile, related searches and any chosen exports. Verify that deleting the profile clears its dependent searches. Do not attach an export to logs or the shared Blueprint Bundle.
